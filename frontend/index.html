Critical Defensive Distinctions (SOC Taxonomy)
Defensive Knowledge Architecture
1. OBSERVATION
A raw piece of telemetry or log entry (e.g., an inbound SYN connection) without confirmed context or malice.
2. INDICATOR (IOC)
An artifact (IP, domain, hash, URL) that may indicate malicious activity. IOC Match ≠ Confirmed Breach.
3. ALERT
A notification generated when telemetry breaches a predefined risk or confidence threshold requiring human triage.
4. THREAT
An adversary or hostile capability possessing the intent and potential to harm an organizational asset.
5. INCIDENT
A confirmed security event that actually compromises confidentiality, integrity, or availability requiring formal response.
